Hearing profile

Measure your own correction

LastWave plays a quiet tone at each frequency, in one ear at a time, and asks whether you heard it. Bands you hear less well are lifted a little; bands that are fine are left alone. About four minutes.

  • •Use headphones, in a quiet room, at low volume. Music is paused for you.
  • •Not a hearing test in the medical sense. No calibrated level, no audiogram — it measures the difference between bands on this phone, through these ear-tips.
  • •If a band is genuinely gone the app will not find it; it corrects within a few dB and caps the lift at +6 dB per band.
  • •Sudden or one-sided hearing loss, or tinnitus: see a doctor before adjusting anything.
