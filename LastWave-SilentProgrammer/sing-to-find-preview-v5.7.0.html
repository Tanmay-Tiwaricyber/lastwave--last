  • Auto-play needs a match over 86%, every sung note matched, and a clear lead — otherwise you tap.
  • Percentages compare tunes, not recordings: key, octave and tempo are ignored on purpose.

Only downloaded / imported audio is searched — reading a stream just to index it is not something the app does, and a catalogue-wide “hum any song on the internet” needs a fingerprint service LastWave has no access to. The first search after a download runs the tune read (one pass per song), then it is cached in the database. Database schema goes 16 → 17 with one new table.
